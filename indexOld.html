About

Alice is a Johannesburg-based artist working primarily in gouache and ink, while exploring a range of other media within her practice. Her work draws on quiet natural forms, with influences from comic books, animation, and traditional art, exploring texture, light, and a sense of stillness through careful, hand-rendered detail.

She produces original works and small print collections, maintaining a considered and process-led approach to image-making.
